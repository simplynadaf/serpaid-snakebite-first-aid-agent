A sleeping-bite emergency · the suction myth

Stops the rescuer from making it worse.

SerpAID · emergency mode
My son was bitten while sleeping on the floor. My brother is sucking the venom out by mouth. My son cannot swallow properly now.
SEVERITY: EMERGENCY
⚠ myth caught
Stop the suction now. It does not remove venom and it harms both of them. The swallowing trouble is a neurotoxic danger sign.

A farm worker, hours from a clinic · the ice-and-wait myth

Ends the wait-and-see that costs limbs.

SerpAID · emergency mode
A worker was bitten two hours ago in the fields. We packed it in ice and are waiting to see if it gets worse. The leg is badly swollen.
SEVERITY: URGENT
⚠ myth caught
Do not wait, and do not use ice. Remove it. Severe swelling means go now, by vehicle, to a hospital with antivenom.
Portuguese · spoken by Polly
