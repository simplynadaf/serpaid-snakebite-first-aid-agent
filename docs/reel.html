Use case 1 · the tourniquet myth

Catches the folklore that kills.

SerpAID · emergency mode
A snake bit my cousin near Pune. Should I tie a tight cloth and go to the village healer? His eyelids feel heavy.
SEVERITY: EMERGENCY
⚠ myth caught
Do not tie a tight cloth, and do not go to a healer first. A tourniquet cuts off blood supply and can cost the limb.
Marathi · spoken by Polly
त्याला शांत ठेवा. काहीही घट्ट बांधू नका, वैद्यकडे जाऊ नका. आता अँटीव्हेनॉमसह रुग्णालयात न्या. 108.
Use case 2 · the cut-and-drain myth

Any language, anywhere.

SerpAID · emergency mode
A farmer was bitten in the field. Can I cut the wound to drain the venom? It will not stop bleeding.
SEVERITY: EMERGENCY
⚠ myth caught
Do not cut the wound. WHO advises against incision. It causes bleeding and infection and removes no venom.
Swahili · spoken by Polly
Usikate jeraha. Endelea bado, ondoa vitu vigumu, na ufikie hospitali na antivenom sasa. Dharura: 999.
